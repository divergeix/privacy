What Stacksift does

Stacksift photographs a business card, reads the text on it using on-device text recognition, and sorts that text into a name, job title, company, phone numbers, email addresses, website and address. You can correct any field, save the result to your phone's contact book, and export your leads as PDF, Excel, Word or CSV.

Everything happens on your phone. There is no Divergeix server and your cards are not uploaded. How your data is handled is set out in the privacy policy, which forms part of these terms.

Recognition is not guaranteed to be correct

This is the most important thing on this page. Text recognition is an estimate, not a transcription. Print quality, lighting, handwriting, logos, unusual layouts and uncommon scripts all change what comes back, and the app will sometimes read a digit, a letter or a whole field wrongly.

Stacksift flags fields it is unsure about, but it cannot flag everything it gets wrong. Check a scanned card before you rely on it — before you dial the number, send the email, or load the row into a CRM.
